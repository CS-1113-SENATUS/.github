# CA Training II | 08/29/26 | Syllo Demo

Thanks everyone for joining tonight! Here's what we covered:

- **App name**: the Course Community app is now called Syllo. It goes live tonight—explore it over the weekend and flag anything that feels hard to find or broken. UX feedback is just as valuable as bug reports.
- **Coursework**: individual exercises release Monday and Wednesday after lecture, group exercises Tuesday and Thursday—this is automated for the semester and doesn't need CA management. Your main responsibility in the app is the Sessions page.
- **Coding tasks**: students get a split view with the problem and a Python coding environment side by side. The Tips button pulls hints from course material without giving away the answer.
- **Attendance**: when lab starts, open Host View and put the QR code on screen—students scan to check in and the code rotates every 30 seconds. You can also mark students present manually if something goes wrong. Late arrivals scan the same code and the system logs their arrival time automatically. There is no checkout QR this semester—if a student leaves early, that's on you to catch, you are the record.
- **Office hours**: clock in when you arrive, clock out when you leave—no session setup, no schedule to configure. Students will be able to see who is currently live.
- **Assign Follow-Up**: use this to route test correction grade updates to @Ian and any Brightspace issues to @Sebastián. It creates a to-do item on their end so nothing falls through the cracks.
